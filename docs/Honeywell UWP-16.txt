The pins (terminals) on a Honeywell UWP (Universal Wall Plate) mounting system define where each low-voltage HVAC wire connects from your heating and cooling system. [1, 2]  
Common Conventional & Power Terminals 

• R / Rc / Rh: 24VAC power. (Note: The UWP uses an internal slider tab to jump R and Rc together for single-transformer systems, or separates them for dual-transformer setups). 
• C: 24VAC Common wire (provides continuous power to smart/digital thermostats). 
• W: Stage 1 Heating (gas, oil, or electric furnace). 
• Y: Stage 1 Compressor/Cooling. 
• G: Blower Fan relay. [3, 4, 5, 6]  

Heat Pump & Advanced Terminals 

• O/B: Changeover/reversing valve for heat pumps. 
• AUX / W2: Auxiliary or second-stage heating. (Important: On UWP-based heat pumps, auxiliary heat connects here rather than the standard W terminal). 
• E: Emergency heat. 
• L / A-A: Heat pump fault input. 
• K: Used for optional C-wire adapters (like the THP9045A). 
• U / S: Specialized terminals for indoor/outdoor wired sensors or ventilation control depending on the specific T-series model. [4, 5, 7, 8, 9]
