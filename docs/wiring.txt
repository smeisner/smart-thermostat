Wiring:

Common:
R/Rc - 24VAC (Supply to send back on other lines. Rc used when separate 24V supply for cooling)
C - 24VAC
G - Fan relay

No heat pump:
W - Heat relay
Y - Cooling relay

Heat Pump: 
W2 - Auxiliary heat
O/B - Reversing valve (both)
Y2 - Second stage cooling

Operation:

Common:
- The thermostat will turn on the fan relay (G) when the thermostat is powered.

No heat pump:
- The thermostat will turn on the heat relay (W) when the thermostat calls for heat.
- The thermostat will turn on the cooling relay (Y) when the thermostat calls for cooling.

With heat pump:
- The thermostat will turn on the auxiliary heat relay (W2) when the thermostat calls for auxiliary heat.
- The thermostat will turn on the reversing valve relay (O/B) when the thermostat calls for heat or cooling.
- The thermostat will turn on the second stage cooling relay (Y2) when the thermostat calls for second stage cooling.
